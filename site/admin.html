👷 Employees & crews

Everyone who logs time or gets timesheet reminders, with or without a sign-in. Link an employee to their account so they can log their own hours, and give each lead their crew. Roles, new accounts and passwords: DCR Desktop 2 → Users. Admins set prices, Estimator and OneDrive upload on Access.

What each sign-in may do beyond its role. Estimator (managers): edits project estimates and change orders on the web and in DCR Desktop 2; ticking it ticks Sees prices too — untick prices for an estimator who works in quantities only. Can upload to OneDrive: adds files to the project's OneDrive folder from Portal 2 (DCR Desktop 2 writes through each office computer's OneDrive and does not ask). A change reaches the person's open pages the next time they load one. The database checks every change; financials, approving payments and sales estimates stay in DCR Desktop 2 → Users.
